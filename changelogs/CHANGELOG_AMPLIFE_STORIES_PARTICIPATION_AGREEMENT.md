# Amplife® Stories Participation Agreement Change Log

**About this log.** This change log identifies the material differences between each published version of the Amplife® Stories Participation Agreement and the version immediately preceding it. It is provided for transparency and reference convenience only; it does not modify, supplement, or interpret any version of the Participation Agreement. The version of the Participation Agreement in effect at the time of your acceptance, as modified in accordance with its terms, governs your participation. If this summary conflicts with the text of any agreement version, the agreement text controls.

**How to read the notes.** Each change carries one or more tags:

- **[NEW TERM]**: provision added that did not appear in the prior version
- **[REVISED]**: existing provision materially modified
- **[REMOVED]**: provision deleted or discontinued
- **[CLARIFIED]**: wording made more precise with no intended change in substance
- **[MECHANICS]**: operational change to a process, procedure, timeline, or point of contact
- **[PROTECTS CUSTOMERS]**: change that expands customer rights, remedies, or disclosures
- **[LEGAL COMPLIANCE]**: change adopted to align with a statute, regulation, or agency rule
- **[DISPUTE RESOLUTION]**: change affecting claims handling, liability, governing law, or arbitration
