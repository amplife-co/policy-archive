# Amplife® Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice Change Log

**About this log.** This change log identifies the material differences between each published version of the Amplife® Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice and the version immediately preceding it. It is provided for transparency and reference convenience only; it does not modify, supplement, or interpret any version of the notice. The version of the notice in effect at the time information was collected governs that collection. If this summary conflicts with the text of any version, the notice text controls.

**How to read the notes.** Each change carries one or more tags:

- **[NEW TERM]**: provision added that did not appear in the prior version
- **[REVISED]**: existing provision materially modified
- **[REMOVED]**: provision deleted or discontinued
- **[CLARIFIED]**: wording made more precise with no intended change in substance
- **[MECHANICS]**: operational change to a process, procedure, timeline, or point of contact
- **[PROTECTS CUSTOMERS]**: change that expands customer rights, remedies, or disclosures
- **[LEGAL COMPLIANCE]**: change adopted to align with a statute, regulation, or agency rule
- **[DISPUTE RESOLUTION]**: change affecting claims handling, liability, governing law, or arbitration

## Version dated July 15, 2026 (Current)

*In effect July 15, 2026 to present · First published version*

- **[NEW TERM] [LEGAL COMPLIANCE] [PROTECTS CUSTOMERS] First publication.** Published July 15, 2026 alongside the Privacy Policy update of the same date. This notice is published as a standalone document, linked separately from the Amplife® Privacy Policy, and describes how Amplife® handles consumer health data as defined by the Washington My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law. It applies in addition to the Privacy Policy.

- **[NEW TERM] [PROTECTS CUSTOMERS] Categories collected and purposes.** Discloses the categories of consumer health data collected, including disability and accessibility preferences volunteered by the customer and health-related information that can be revealed by purchase records, together with the purposes for which each category is used.

- **[NEW TERM] [PROTECTS CUSTOMERS] Consumer health data rights.** States the rights that attach to consumer health data, including the right to confirm collection, to access, to withdraw consent, and to request deletion, and the process for exercising them.

- **[NEW TERM] Sharing and no-sale commitment.** Describes the limited circumstances in which consumer health data is shared with service providers and states that Amplife® does not sell consumer health data.

- **[MECHANICS] Contact and archive.** Requests are directed to hello@amplife.co and prior versions are available in the policy version archive.
