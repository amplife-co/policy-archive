# 7-16-26  

Last Updated: July 16th, 2026

The amount of money required to complete the purchase of a Listing is the Purchase Price, as defined in the [Sell with Amplife® Service Terms](https://help.amplife.co/en-US/sell-with-amplife-service-terms-524172).

Vendors are responsible for setting the Purchase Prices of their Listings on Amplife®.

We may review the Purchase Prices of Listings on Amplife®, including shipping charges, and compare them with other prices available to our customers. If we identify pricing practices on a Listing that harm customer trust, we can remove the Listing and, in serious or repeated cases, suspend and/or terminate your Amplife® account, as provided in Sections 15(c) and 3 of the [Amplife® Vendor Participation Agreement](https://help.amplife.co/en-US/amplife-vendor-participation-agreement-537645) ("Agreement").

Pricing practices that harm customer trust include, but are not limited to:

  - Setting a reference price on a Listing that misleads customers;
  - Setting a Purchase Price on a Listing that is significantly higher than recent prices for that Listing on Amplife®;
  - Selling multiple units of a Listing for more per unit than that of a single unit of the same Listing; and
  - Setting an excessive shipping fee. Amplife® considers current public carrier rates, reasonable handling charges, as well as buyer perception when determining whether a shipping charge violates our Fair Pricing Policy.

This policy is a [Program Policy](https://help.amplife.co/en-US/articles/program-policies-140786) under the Agreement. If anything in this policy conflicts with the Agreement or the applicable Service Terms, the Agreement and Service Terms control.
