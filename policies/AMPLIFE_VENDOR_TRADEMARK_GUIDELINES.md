## No Approval Needed: Saying You Sell on Amplife®

Under Section 11.3 of the Agreement, you do not need prior approval from Amplife® to:

  - Accurately state that you sell on Amplife®, for example, "We sell on Amplife®" or "Our products are available at amplife.co"; and
  - Use the plain-text Amplife® name and the Badge in accordance with these Guidelines to advertise that your listings are available on an Amplife® website.

If you comply with these Guidelines, you do not need any further approval from Amplife® for these uses.

## Advance Written Permission Required

The following always require advance written permission from Amplife® (contact hello@amplife.co):

  - Press releases;
  - Co-branded campaigns; and
  - Any statement or material that states or implies a partnership with, or sponsorship or endorsement by, Amplife®.

You must not use the Amplife logo or any other Amplife® trademark, other than the plain-text Amplife® name and the Badge as permitted by these Guidelines, in your online or offline marketing materials (including listings on Amplife® websites) unless you have written permission from Amplife®.

You will not misrepresent or embellish the relationship between you and Amplife®. Amplife® may require you to promptly correct or remove any inaccurate, misleading, or non-compliant statement or material, and you will do so promptly.

## Proper Form for the Amplife® Name

  - Spell the name exactly as "Amplife" with a capital A, and include the ® symbol on the first or most prominent use, for example, "Amplife®". Do not abbreviate, hyphenate, translate, or misspell the name.
  - Use the name as a brand name (for example, "available on Amplife®"), never as a verb or in the possessive form.
  - Do not combine the name with your own names or marks, and do not use it in or as any part of your business name, product name, domain name, social media handle, or app name.
  - Do not use anything confusingly similar to the Amplife® name or any other Mark.
  - Include the following attribution statement in and on any materials that display the Badge or make more than incidental use of the Amplife® name (online or offline): "Amplife and the Amplife logo are trademarks of AMPLIFE LLC, registered in the U.S."

3.  You must use the Badge solely for the purpose expressly authorized by Amplife® in these Guidelines, and your use must:
4.  Comply with the most up-to-date version of these Guidelines; and
5.  Comply with any other terms, conditions, or policies that Amplife® may issue from time to time that apply to the use of the Badge.
