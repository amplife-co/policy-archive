# **What is Personal Information**

For the purpose of this Privacy Policy, "Personal Information" means any data that relates to an identified or identifiable individual or that is linked or linkable to them by Amplife®, no matter where the individual lives. This means that data that directly identifies you, such as your name, is Personal Information, and also data that does not directly identify you but that can reasonably be used to identify you, such as your device identifiers, is Personal Information. Aggregated data is considered non-personal data for the purposes of this Privacy Policy.

"Sensitive Personal Information" means Personal Information that reveals information about a disability or health condition, or other categories defined as sensitive under applicable law, including the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). Some disability and health information is also "consumer health data" under state health-privacy laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act.

# **Cookie Policy**

When you visit our Website, we place an alphanumeric identifier text file called a "cookie" in the browser directory of your computer's hard drive to enable our systems to recognize your browser and to provide features and storage of your preferences. A cookie is a small piece of information that a website can store on your web browser and later retrieve.

We use the following types of cookies:

  - Strictly necessary cookies: Required for the Website to operate. These are essential to core Site functionality and are not subject to opt-out.

  - Functional cookies: Enable personalized features and preferences. You may turn these off through the "Manage preferences" link in our cookie banner or in your browser settings.

  - Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use the Website so we can improve it. This information is used in aggregated form. You may opt out at any time through the "Manage preferences" link in our cookie banner.

The Website does not use advertising or ad-platform pixels.

We also work with third-party analytics providers who use cookies to collect traffic data. These providers act as our service providers under written contracts that limit their use of the information to providing services to us. This information is used in aggregated and anonymized form to analyze usage of our Website and to improve the operation of our Website, Services and business generally.

If you choose to block cookies through the cookie banner or your browser, some pages of the Website may not function properly. For more information on managing cookies, visit [www.allaboutcookies.org](https://www.allaboutcookies.org).
