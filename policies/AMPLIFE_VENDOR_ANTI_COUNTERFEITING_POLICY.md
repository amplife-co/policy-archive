We may remove suspected listings based on our review of listings. We also work with rights holders and law enforcement worldwide to take and support legal action against vendors who knowingly violate this policy and harm our customers. To help keep the shopping experience trustworthy for our customers, by selling or advertising on Amplife®, you agree that:

  - The sale or advertisement of counterfeit listings is strictly prohibited;
  - You may not sell or advertise any listings that are not legal for sale, such as listings that have been illegally replicated, reproduced, or manufactured; and
  - You must provide records about the authenticity of your listings if Amplife® requests that documentation.

Failure to abide by this policy may result in funds being withheld as described above, suspension and/or termination of your Amplife® account, and other legal consequences.
